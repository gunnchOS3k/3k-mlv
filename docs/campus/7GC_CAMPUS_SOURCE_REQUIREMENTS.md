# 7GC Digital Campus Source Requirements

These requirements are extracted from the owner's seven UPNOW Campus Urban Planning Briefs and are canonical for the 3k MLV digital-campus implementation.

## Fidelity rule

"1:1 digital version" means:

1. Every brief-defined room, program, functional zone, and operating constraint gets a traceable digital representation.
2. The digital twin preserves each site's local planning thesis instead of using one generic building with different colors.
3. Where the brief defines pilot, semi-permanent, and full-campus stages, expose those phases as planning layers.
4. Never imply that a physical campus, partnership, exact site, or facility exists when the brief does not support that claim.
5. Where exact geometry is absent, use an authored navigable layout labeled as a digital planning twin rather than pretending it is surveyed architecture.
6. Every digital room/zone records source brief, source section, phase, requirement ID, digital route/object, and evidence status.

Required artifacts:
- `docs/campus/7GC_CAMPUS_SOURCE_REQUIREMENTS.md`
- `artifacts/campus/7GC_CAMPUS_REQUIREMENT_TRACEABILITY.json`
- `artifacts/campus/7GC_CAMPUS_DIGITAL_TWIN_COVERAGE.json`

Coverage states:

```text
IMPLEMENTED_SPATIALLY
IMPLEMENTED_FUNCTIONALLY
IMPLEMENTED_BOTH
PLANNED_NOT_YET_IMPLEMENTED
NOT_APPLICABLE
BLOCKED_BY_REAL_WORLD_VALIDATION
```

A campus may claim `BRIEF_DIGITAL_COVERAGE=100%` only when every brief requirement is represented or truthfully classified.
